About the Developer

Capstone Life treats real estate as a passion rather than a business. The Bengaluru developer is run by a team of technocrats from IIT and IIM backgrounds who have worked at leading technology companies, and it aims to bring transparency, collaboration and rigour to how homes are built and sold.

Sustainability, technology and design sit at the centre of every Capstone Life community. The Midsummer Rain is its lakeside villa community off Airport Road, registered with Karnataka RERA under PRM/KA/RERA/1250/303/PR/030924/006983.
